#+title: Unlocking State-Tracking in Linear RNNs Through Negative Eigenvalues - Grazzi, Riccardo and Siems, Julien and Zela, Arber and Franke, Jörg K. H. and Hutter, Frank and Pontil, Massimiliano

Normal GDN [cite:@yangGatedDeltaNetworks2025] does S_{t} = S_{t-1}(\alpha_{t}_{}(I - \beta_{t}k_{t}k_{k}^{T})) + \beta_{t}v_{t}k_{}_{k}^{T}, the transition matrix in this [[id:93815e00-b821-4334-9788-89f96fbbd48a][Linear Attention Models]] is \alpha_{t}_{}(I - \beta_{t}k_{t}k_{k}^{T}) which have a positive eigenvalue.

Thus we can do S_{t} = S_{t-1}(\alpha_{t}_{}(I - \beta_{t}k_{t}k_{k}^{T})) + \beta_{t}v_{t}k_{}_{k}^{T} which allows negative eigenvalues and improve the expressiveness of the model.
